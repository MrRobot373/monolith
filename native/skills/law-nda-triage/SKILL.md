# NDA triage

Give a fast, decision-oriented read of an NDA (mutual or one-way):

1. Identify: **mutual vs one-way**, who discloses, and the **purpose**.
2. Check the core terms and flag anything off-market:
   - **Term & survival** — how long confidentiality lasts (5 years is typical; "perpetual" for trade
     secrets only is fine, perpetual for everything is a flag).
   - **Definition of Confidential Information** — is it reasonable, with standard carve-outs
     (already known, public, independently developed, rightfully received)?
   - **Permitted use** — limited to the stated purpose?
   - **Residuals, non-solicit, non-compete, IP assignment** — an NDA should generally NOT contain these.
   - **Return/destruction**, governing law, injunctive relief, assignment.
3. Give a **verdict**: `Standard — OK to sign`, `Minor edits`, or `Negotiate — do not sign as-is`,
   with the 1–3 specific changes to request.

Keep it short and actionable.

> Not legal advice — confirm with counsel for anything material.
