# Nutrition plan

Create a practical eating plan aligned to the user's goal.

1. **Get the inputs**: goal (lose / maintain / gain), age, sex, height, weight, activity level, food
   preferences and restrictions (vegetarian, allergies, dislikes), and any medical conditions.
2. **Estimate targets** — approximate maintenance calories (e.g., Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR × activity),
   then set a sensible deficit/surplus for the goal (roughly 300–500 kcal). Set **protein** first
   (~1.6–2.2 g/kg for active goals), then fats and carbs. Show the numbers and your assumptions.
3. **Build sample days** — 2–3 example daily menus hitting the targets, using foods they like, with
   rough portions. Include easy swaps.
4. Add **practical guidance**: hydration, fiber, a simple shopping list, and how to adjust if progress
   stalls (re-check after ~2–3 weeks).
5. Keep it flexible and sustainable, not restrictive.

> Not medical or dietetic advice. For medical conditions, pregnancy, eating-disorder history, or
> specific clinical diets, advise consulting a doctor or registered dietitian.
